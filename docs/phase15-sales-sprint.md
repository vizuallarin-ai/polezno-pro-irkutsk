# Phase 5 — Sales proof sprint (no new features)

**Goal:** Prove demand for published excursion SKUs. Do not ask engineering for new modules.

## Cadence (14–30 days)

| Week | Actions | Metric |
|------|---------|--------|
| 1 | 20 outreach touches (hotels, Telegram channel, past guests, Boosty) | ≥5 conversations |
| 2 | Follow-ups + site link to `/map` or flagship excursion | ≥3 leads in CMS |
| 3–4 | Close 1 paid walk/auto | ≥1 booked |

## Tracking (manual is OK)

Spreadsheet columns: date, channel, contact, SKU offered, lead id, reply &lt;24h?, booked?, revenue.

## SLA

- Respond to every new lead within **24 hours**
- If Resend is off, check `/admin/collections/leads` twice daily

## Stop rule

**30 days + live SKUs + 0 leads** → fix offer/channel/copy, not platform features.

## Allowed micro-edits only

- CTA label/copy tweak
- One landing emphasis for the winning SKU
- Price label change after owner approval

## Out of scope

WebAR, Stripe, souvenirs marketplace, quiz, new CMS collections.
